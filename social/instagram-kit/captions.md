### Day 2 · pin: Axon TMS, plan to exit

```
Most truck yards still run on phone calls and paper. Queues at the window, and nobody sees the whole yard.

Axon TMS puts every truck on one live record, from plan to exit:

01 Plan: loads come in from your ERP before the gate opens
02 Check-in: drivers scan a QR code on their own phone. No app, no login
03 Approve: one click, and the driver gets a QR slip
04 Gate: plate and container matched against the plan
05 Warehouse: dock calls and loading timers
06 Documents: CMR checklist and automatic release slips

Gate, warehouse and office see the same picture, on desk, wallboard and phone. Live today.
```

How it works

A day in the yard, end to end.

Follow one truck, from planned to released. Seven steps, each owned by exactly one desk, each leaving a timestamp.

1 Planning

The day arrives pre planned.

Loads land from your ERP or a CSV import, every truck, plate and time window, ready before the gate opens.

  • Import via REST, SFTP or Excel
  • One governed record per truck
  • Filter by date, status, customer or plate
2 Driver

The driver checks in from a QR.

No app, no login. The driver scans a code and confirms reference, plate and container, with zero PII exposure.

  • Public, tokenized check in page
  • Confirms reference, plate & container
  • Status updates by email
3 Approval · self check in

Operations approves the check in.

Each self check in lands in the Approval Inbox. An operator approves or rejects it, the first action wins, so only verified loads move forward.

  • Every self check in becomes an approval request
  • First operator action wins, approved or rejected
  • An approval email + QR slip goes back to the driver
4 Gate

Gate matches identifiers and admits.

Plate and container are matched against the plan. Any mismatch is flagged before the truck moves, and the arrival is timestamped.

  • Planned vs detected plate & container
  • Mismatch & unknown arrival flags
  • One deliberate action, Mark Arrived
5 Warehouse

Called to the dock, then loaded.

The warehouse calls the truck to its ramp and runs loading on a live timer the whole floor can read, Ready at Dock, Loading, Loaded.

  • Call to Dock → Start → Finish Loading
  • Live per slot loading timer
  • Dock queue pressure at a glance
6 Documents

Documents cleared, CMR issued.

Upload proof and mark documents ready. The branded CMR generates itself, and the driver is notified the moment docs are cleared.

  • Multi file upload · required docs checklist
  • Template driven CMR, stamped & signed
  • Mark Docs Ready notifies the driver
7 Release · email

Released, and auto emailed to everyone.

Release is recorded in the full slot. Then a branded email, with the approval and a PDF packet, lands with the driver, the customer and customs. Nobody chases paperwork at six in the evening.

  • Exit / Release recorded in the full slot
  • Branded PDF packet + QR release slip
  • Auto emailed to driver, customer & customs
One sealed record

Everything ends up in one place.

When the truck leaves, the work doesn't vanish into a spreadsheet. It becomes a governed record, timestamped, tagged and defensible.

The full slot

Open it, and see the whole life of the load.

A timestamp on every state change, every document, every tag and special note, the transport acceptance and any damage claims, plus a tamper evident audit trail of who did what, and when.

  • A timestamp for every action, Arrived, Loaded, Exited
  • Tags, priority & special notes shown to every desk
  • Frozen & exportable, CSV and branded PDF
Claims

Claims, reviewed before release.

When something arrives damaged, log a claim with photo proof and resolve it in one inbox. Release stays blocked until it's cleared, so the dispute never leaves the yard.

  • Damage claims logged on the slot
  • Photo proof & a review workflow
  • Release blocked until resolved
Branding & policies

Make it yours. Keep it compliant.

Your logo on every PDF, your CMR template, your vocabulary of tags, plus sign in policy, station PINs, 2FA, GDPR terms and a complete audit log, all from one settings shell.

  • Branding · logo, colours & CMR template
  • Sign in policy, station PINs & 2FA
  • GDPR terms · full audit & activity log
Wallboard

The pulse of the yard, on every screen.

A 15 second refresh wallboard that gives gate, warehouse and documents the same operational truth as the back office. No desk required.

In the spirit of honesty

What Axon doesn't do.

A short list. We're better at running the yard because we don't try to be everything.

Not

An ERP replacement

We don't own purchase orders, invoicing or master data. Your ERP keeps that crown, Axon hands it a clean, signed handoff at the slot boundary.

Not

A route optimizer

We don't pick lanes, plan multi stops, or quote freight. We govern what happens once a truck arrives at your yard, that's the slice we do well.

Not

A carrier appointment system

Booking slots with carriers happens upstream of us. Axon takes the appointment as input and runs it from the moment the truck rolls through your gate.

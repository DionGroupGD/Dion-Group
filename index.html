Questions

Before we begin.

The right starting point depends on your idea, your organisation and the people it needs to serve.

Should I choose a product or a custom project?

Axon TMS supports yard operations. Aegis, our AI product for document processing, is in development. For a different workflow, website or application, we can discuss a custom build.

Can we discuss a public-sector project?

Yes. Share your brief and any procurement, accessibility, security or hosting requirements. We will discuss the scope and assess whether we can meet the specific needs of the project.

What will it cost, and how long will it take?

Both depend on scope, integrations and delivery requirements. After an initial discussion, we propose an approach, estimated budget and timeline. Any changes are discussed as the project develops.

Where does AI fit into a project?

Where it serves a clear purpose: extracting information, assisting a task or reducing repetitive work. We discuss data handling, limitations and where human review is needed before choosing an approach.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. Dion Group is based in Greece and works internationally. We can discuss your project in English, German or Greek, and agree a practical way to collaborate across locations.

What happens to ownership and support?

For custom projects, we agree code ownership, licences, hosting, documentation and support in the project scope. Dion products have their own terms. These details are part of the conversation from the start.

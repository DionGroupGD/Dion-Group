Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies (such as your browser’s local storage) dion-group.com uses, why, and how you can change your choice. Anything that is not strictly necessary is only used after you allow it.

2. Strictly necessary

  • dion_cookie_consent · Dion Group · local storage · until you change your choice or clear your browser storage. Remembers the choices you made in the cookie banner, so we don’t ask again on every page.

This storage is needed to respect your choice, so it is used without consent.

3. Preferences (only with your consent)

  • dion_lang · Dion Group · local storage · until you withdraw consent or clear your browser storage. Remembers the language you chose (English, German or Greek) on this device.

4. Analytics (only with your consent)

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are used, so we can improve the website. It is only loaded after you allow “Analytics” in the cookie banner. Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

  • _ga · Google · cookie · 2 years. Distinguishes visitors.
  • _ga_1CRLCBS70Z · Google · cookie · 2 years. Keeps the state of the current visit.

When you withdraw consent, we stop Google Analytics on the page and delete these cookies.

5. Contact forms

The contact form and the Axon pricing form are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). When you send a form, your browser goes to FormSubmit, which may show a short security check (CAPTCHA) and set its own cookies on formsubmit.co. FormSubmit’s policies apply to that step.

6. What we do not use

No advertising cookies and no social-media tracking. Fonts and videos are hosted on our own server, so loading the website sends no data to font or video providers.

7. Changing your choice

Use the cookie settings button in the bottom-left corner of every page to change or withdraw your consent at any time. You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings; we will then ask again.

8. Changes

We update this policy when the website changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. How we handle personal data in general is explained in our Privacy Notice.
